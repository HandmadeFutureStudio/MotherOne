Mother
One

Read her backstory then cast your vote for or against the 'what if?' she represents.

For centuries, control over conception has been seized and weaponized — compulsory pregnancy, tracked and punished, a property of the state rather than the body carrying it. Every method ever built to prevent pregnancy — a pill, a device, a law — could always be overridden by force.

What if dormant traits were unlocked that allowed humans to control their own reproduction? Mother One is a symbol from a future in which research into the animal world has led to such breakthroughs in biological sovereignty.

Mother One is depicted holding a cat because, like domestic and wild cats, humans used to ovulate upon orgasm, but evolved towards a fixed cycle of ovulation. With that function restored, humans could control their own reproduction without outside intervention, making consent and desire central to reproduction/pregnancy.

Would you want to live in a world where reproductive choice is protected by scientific intervention in our human biology? Explore the real and imagined timeline then place a pill for your vote.

Timeline I · Documented History

documented history     speculative future history

  • 1933
    Researchers isolate the hormone progesterone in pure form.
  • 1953
    Biologist and suffragist Katharine McCormick funds the research for the first contraceptive pill.
  • 1957
    The FDA approves the pill (Enovid) for use in period cramps.
  • 1973
    SCOTUS decides Roe v. Wade, paving the way for legal abortion.
  • 1976
    The Hyde Amendment bars federal funds from covering abortion except in narrow cases.
  • 1992
    Planned Parenthood v. Casey empowers state restrictions on access to abortion, narrowing Roe's protections.
  • 2016
    Base gene editing is developed: a precision technique correcting a single DNA letter without cutting the DNA strand.
  • 2021
    Texas enacts SB 8, banning abortion after six weeks and deputizing private citizens to enforce it.
  • 2022
    SCOTUS overturns Roe v. Wade in Dobbs v. Jackson Women's Health Organization.
  • 2022–25
    17 states enact fetal personhood laws in the wake of Dobbs. Congressional Republicans reintroduce a federal statute to protect fetal-life from conception (Life at Conception Act).
  • 2025
    The first bespoke in vivo gene therapy corrects a fatal genetic disorder, proving personalized, single-infusion gene editing works in a living person.

Timeline II · Speculative Future History

  • 2032
    The first fully AI-designed gene-editing enzyme corrects the strongest known genetic risk factor for Alzheimer's disease.
  • 2045
    Gene-editing reactivates orgasm-triggered-ovulation in humans, seen in cats.

    Gene-editing grants humans sperm-storage and selective fertilization, seen in birds.

Seed of this Idea

I came up with the seed of this idea when I was fifteen, living in Southern Idaho in a fundamentalist Mormon community. I wasn't yet sexually active, and I hadn't yet come out to myself as queer. But I already understood quite sharply the danger of patriarchal control over conception, of how much power was placed in the hands of men, and how little was left in the hands of the body carrying a possible pregnancy. I asked myself, what if conception was only possible alongside orgasm?

I wrote an early version of this idea as a creative writing assignment for my English teacher. I didn't have the language yet for bodily autonomy, and I imagined a way biology itself could be a solution. I got in trouble for writing a vulgar story.

Returning to this idea now with speculative design as the context has been a meaningful exploration.
